# Carousel

Not a video: 7 photos a viewer swipes. The cover asks a question about a list people know, the 5 ranks answer it from
the 5th to the 1st, and the last photo shows the game's address. An app maker grew on TikTok with no ads with this
format: a list, a question, and the name on the last photo alone.

- **Pick one of the facts' three `lists`** and tell its first 5, in its order, the 1st first: each list is ranked
  already, so the ranks are the facts', never yours.
  - `fight-the-most`: the flags that took the most tiles from other flags and lost the most to them since
    `foughtSince`, ranked by the two together. A rank's numbers are the tiles it `took` and those it `lost`, a line
    each: the line under them says its `rival` ("MOSTLY AGAINST FRANCE").
  - `far-from-home`: the flags whose land is the farthest from their own country. A rank's number is its `km`: the
    line says where it is ("FIGHTING IN INDONESIA").
  - `biggest-empires`: the flags holding the most of the world. A rank's number is its `share` of the world: the line
    says what it holds ("OF THE WORLD").
- **Pick the list a stranger stops for**, and vary: read `told`, where each carousel has its `list` and `ranks`. A
  list whose 5 are the 5 of its last post is not told again.
- **`hook`: the cover**, the question or the claim the ranks answer, in big words over the globe: "THE 5 COUNTRIES
  THAT FIGHT THE MOST", "WHICH COUNTRY FIGHTS THE MOST?". Upper case, 20 to 50 letters. It is TikTok's title over the
  photos too. Say only what the list holds: `fight-the-most` counts from `foughtSince`.
- **`ranks`: 5**, from the 1st to the 5th, each the `code` of its entry, its flag, and a `line` of words under its
  name, upper case, 25 letters at most, with no digit: the photo draws the number beside it.
- **No call to play, and no address**: the last photo has it, and a call in the middle of a carousel cuts its views.
- **In English**, always.
