# Carousel

Not a video: 7 photos a viewer swipes. The cover asks a question about a list people know, the 5 ranks answer it from
the 5th to the 1st, and the last photo shows the game's address. An app maker grew on TikTok with no ads with this
format: a list, a question, and the name on the last photo alone.

- **Pick one of the facts' four `lists`** and tell its first 5, in its order, the 1st first: each list is ranked
  already, so the ranks are the facts', never yours.
  - `own-land`: the countries whose own flag holds the least of their own ground. A rank's number is the share the
    `holder` holds: the line names it ("HELD BY PALAU").
  - `most-attacked`: the flags the most tiles were taken from since `attackedSince`. A rank's number is the tiles
    `lost`: the line says who took them ("TAKEN BY TURKEY").
  - `far-from-home`: the flags whose land is the farthest from their own country. A rank's number is its `km`: the
    line says where it is ("FIGHTING IN INDONESIA").
  - `biggest-empires`: the flags holding the most of the world. A rank's number is its `share` of the world: the line
    says what it holds ("OF THE WORLD").
- **Pick the list a stranger stops for**, and vary: read `told`, where each carousel has its `list` and `ranks`. A
  list whose 5 are the 5 of its last post is not told again.
- **`hook`: the cover**, the question or the claim the ranks answer, in big words over the globe: "THESE 5 COUNTRIES
  DON'T OWN THEIR OWN LAND", "WHO IS THE INTERNET ATTACKING THE MOST?". Upper case, 20 to 50 letters. It is TikTok's
  title over the photos too. Say only what the list holds: `most-attacked` counts from `attackedSince`.
- **`ranks`: 5**, from the 1st to the 5th, each the `code` of its entry (the country of `own-land`, the flag of the
  others) and a `line` of words under its name, upper case, 25 letters at most, with no digit: the photo draws the
  number beside it.
- **No call to play, and no address**: the last photo has it, and a call in the middle of a carousel cuts its views.
- **In English**, always.
