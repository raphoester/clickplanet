# Far invasion

The season's best clips show a flag far from home: Hungary in Indonesia (30,789 views on YouTube, ten times the next
best), Palestine in Antarctica, Bulgaria in South America, Canada and France in Southeast Asia, Poland in South Africa.
A stranger stops for a country where it has no business being.

- **Tell a flag fighting on a continent that is not its own**: the flag's own `continent` is not the place's. The
  farther and the stranger the better: a European flag in Southeast Asia beats one next door, and a neighbour is not
  far. Any kind of a front, a place or a saga will do, as long as the flag on screen is the far one.
- **`hook`: the question a stranger asks** of the flag and the place on screen: "WHY IS HUNGARY IN INDONESIA?", "WHAT
  IS POLAND DOING IN SOUTH AFRICA?". Upper case, 20 to 40 letters, ending with "?". It shows alone over the first second
  and a half; then the headline answers it.
- **`headline`: the answer**, plain and true when the clip ends, as every headline is: "HUNGARY INVADES INDONESIA".
  Never the hook again, and never "VS".
- **The place is where the flag fights**, never where it comes from.
- **`look`: `dive`** for a front or a place, so the viewer sees the far flag's tiles up close.
- `language`: "en".
