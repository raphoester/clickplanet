Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

ClickPlanet is a game made by Raphaël Oester. You can play without an account, and to play we never ask for your name, email or any other contact detail. You can choose to sign in with Google, Discord or your email address. This page says what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

Data Why Kept for
Your IP address and browser user agent, in our server's access log Security, debugging and finding bots 14 days
Each tile you take: the tile, the flag, the time, and your account Season scores, titles, and undoing cheaters Until your account is deleted; the tile, the flag and the time then stay, with no account. A guest account is deleted 90 days after your last visit.
The IP address (or its /64 for IPv6) each tile was taken from Undoing and stopping cheaters 72 hours
How you click: timing and order of clicks, map reads, bonus catches, linked to your IP address Automatic bot detection 72 hours
A ban record, keyed on an IP address (or /64), if you are banned for cheating Keeping cheaters out Indefinitely
A guest account: a random id, when it was created and last used, and a cookie (cp_sid) that identifies it. We store only a hash of the cookie. Knowing that the same browser is still playing 90 days after your last visit
Only if you sign in with Google or Discord: the id that service gives you, your email address if the service says it is verified, and when you signed in with it. It is linked to your account. Keeping your account on every device where you sign in, and finding the same account when you sign in with the same verified address another way Until you delete your account
Only if you sign in with your email address: the address, and when you signed in with it. It is linked to your account. The code we send is kept for 10 minutes in an encrypted cookie in your browser, not on our server. To limit how many codes go to one address, our server also keeps the address in memory for up to one hour after a code is sent. Signing you in, and keeping your account on every device where you sign in Until you delete your account
Chat messages: the name you chose, the text, your flag, a random id your browser keeps, your IP address and user agent Showing the chat, and moderating it 30 days
Chat reactions: the message, the reaction, and who gave it: your account if you have a username, otherwise the short tag made from your IP address Counting reactions, and letting you take yours off 30 days

Tiles you hold stay on the map with their flag until someone takes them. The map records a flag per tile, not who clicked it.

The legal basis for all of this is our legitimate interest in running a fair game and keeping it safe from abuse.

What stays in your browser

Your browser keeps some settings in local storage: the country you play for, your sound settings, and your chat name and id. They are not sent to us, except the chat name and id when you post a message. Clearing your site data removes them.

Cookies

We set one cookie, cp_sid, which holds your account. It is strictly necessary to play: we do not use it for advertising or analytics. When you sign in, it lasts 30 days after your last visit. While you sign in, we also set cp_oauth for 10 minutes, to check that the sign-in you finish is the one you started. We use no tracking or analytics cookies.

Who else sees your data

  • Cloudflare delivers the site and runs Turnstile, the check that you are not a bot. Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to do this. If you sign in with your email address, Cloudflare also sends the email with your code, so it sees the address and the email. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts the page uses, so your browser asks Google for them. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google and Discord, only if you sign in with them. That service sees that you sign in to ClickPlanet, and sends us the data in the table above. See Google's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy.
  • Everyone playing sees chat messages: the name, the text, the flag, and a short tag made from a salted hash of your IP address. The tag does not reveal your address. They also see how many people gave each reaction to a message, but not who.

The Discord and Buy Me a Coffee buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to those services until you click them. We do not sell your data.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we ask Google for two things only: the id Google gives your account, and your email address with whether Google has verified it. We do not ask for your name, photo, contacts or any other Google data.

  • How we use it: the id finds your ClickPlanet account when you sign in again. The email is kept only if Google says it is verified. It finds the same account when you sign in with that address another way, with an email code or with Discord, and lets us contact you about your account if we need to.
  • Who we share it with: nobody. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • How long we keep it: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes the id and the email at once.

ClickPlanet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK or a place with similar laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. If you signed in, you can delete your account yourself: open the menu, then Account, then Delete account. For everything else we do not know who you are, so email us your IP address and roughly when you played so we can find it. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

ClickPlanet is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page and the date at the top.
