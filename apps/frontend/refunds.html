Refunds

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page says when we refund a payment for a regional flag. The terms of sale say what you buy.

When we refund

  • We reject your flag after you paid for it: we refund everything you paid for it, automatically, and stop its renewal.
  • A payment buys no time, for example one that lands after your flag was rejected: we refund it in full, automatically.
  • You change your mind before your flag opens: write to us, and we refund everything you paid for it.
  • We close your flag because it breaks the terms of service or the review rules: we refund the time not used.
  • We shut down the game, or stop the regional flags: we refund the time not used.
  • You paid twice by mistake for the same flag: write to us within 14 days, and we refund the second payment.

In other cases, we do not refund time already used.

How a refund works

A refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, through Stripe.

A full refund takes back the time it paid for, and so does a chargeback (when you ask your bank to take back a payment). Your flag then ends sooner, and it may close at once.

Stripe, through Link, may also refund a payment in some cases, within 60 days of it.

How to ask

Email privacy@clickplanet.lol with your flag's name and your receipt. You can also ask Link's support at link.com.
