What you buy

You buy time for one regional flag. Until its end date, the flag shows in the game's list of flags, and players can pick it and play for it. You hold the flag while you pay for it: until its end date, only you can add time to it.

We do not promise any land, tiles, players, rank or result. Anyone can take your flag's land, as with every other flag.

Pay, then review

You pay when you send your flag. We then review it. Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, checks it first, often in a few seconds; when it is not sure, a person of our team decides. Its time starts when it is approved, and the flag opens at once.

If your flag is rejected, we refund everything you paid for it, automatically, and stop its renewal. If you are still on the payment page, it closes before you pay. A person reviews only flags that are paid.

Think a rejection is wrong? Email support@clickplanet.lol and a person looks at your flag again.

Plans and prices

  • 1 week: €4, paid once.
  • 1 month: €10, paid again at the start of each month until you stop it.
  • 1 year: €60, paid again at the start of each year until you stop it.

Prices include VAT. Each payment adds its period to your flag's end date. The first one counts from the day we approve the flag.

Stopping a renewal

You can stop a monthly or yearly plan at any time, in the game or through Link. No more payments are taken, and the time you already paid for stays.

When the time ends

At the end date, the flag closes. Its land stays on the map, and its players pick another flag. Then any signed-in player can buy time for it, you too. The first one who pays holds it from then: the flag opens again with its name, picture and land, with no new review. While a monthly or yearly plan still tries to renew it, for example while Stripe tries a card again, nobody else can buy it.

If someone else buys time for a flag you held, a payment of your old plan that comes later buys nothing: we refund it in full, automatically, and stop that plan.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe, through Link, which acts as the merchant of record. This means that Link is the seller on your receipt. It charges the VAT, sends the receipts and answers questions about payments.

When we remove a flag

We can remove a flag that breaks the terms of service or the review rules: for example a hateful flag, or a flag that is not the flag of a place. It closes for good: nobody can buy time for it, and a new flag can take its name. We tell its holder why, in the game. Then we refund the time not used.

If we shut down the game, or stop the regional flags, we refund the time not used. The refunds page says how refunds work.

Your right to cancel

In the EU, you normally have 14 days to cancel a purchase. Until your flag opens, you can cancel and get a full refund: email us. A flag's time starts as soon as we approve it, or at once when you add time to a flag that is open, or buy time for a flag whose time ended. So before Stripe's payment page, we ask you to confirm that you want it to start then, and that you lose your right to cancel once it starts.
