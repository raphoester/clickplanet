Paint the planet with your flag

A free multiplayer game in your browser. Pick a country, then click tiles on a 3D globe to paint them with its flag. Everyone plays on the same planet, live.

Play now Discord

Sign in, click 2× faster No account needed to play. Sign in with Google or Discord to get your clicks back twice as fast, and keep your account on every device.

How it works

Three steps to world domination

  1. 1

    Pick a country

    Play for your own country, or any other. You can change sides whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Click the globe

    Every click paints a tile with your flag. Drag to turn the planet, zoom in to aim.

  3. 3

    Hold the ground

    A tile stays yours until someone takes it. Countries fight for the most ground, live.

Leaderboard

Watch the ranks move

Every country, its tiles and its share of the map, updated as players click around the world.

The map

Whole continents, flag by flag

  • Bonus boxes

    Catch one to keep a bomb, spread clicks, a refill of your clicks, or the power to close a shape and take what is inside. Use it when you choose.

  • Live chat

    Talk with everyone on the planet while you play. Make alliances, or argue about borders.

  • The leader's anthem

    The national anthem of the country in first place plays while you click. Take the lead to change the music.

  • Take a picture

    Photograph the globe with your country's badge on it, and share it with the link to join you.

Anywhere

On your phone, on your computer

Nothing to install. Open the page and play. Sign in with Google or Discord when you want your account on every device.
