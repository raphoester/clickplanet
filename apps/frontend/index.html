How it works

Three steps to world domination

  1. 1

    Pick a country

    Play for your own country, or any other. You can change sides whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Click the globe

    Every click paints a tile with your flag. Drag to turn the planet, zoom in to aim.

  3. 3

    Hold the ground

    A tile stays yours until someone takes it. Countries fight for the most ground, live.

The board

Country against country

Every tile you paint counts for your country. The board ranks every flag on the planet and moves with every click.

  • First place plays its anthem to every player.
  • Each season ends with a Final Battle.
