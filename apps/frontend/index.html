ClickPlanet

A free multiplayer game in your browser. Pick a country, then click tiles on a 3D globe to paint them with its flag.

Everyone plays on the same planet, live. Each country fights for the most ground, and a tile stays yours until someone takes it.

No account needed. Sign in with Google or Discord to keep your account on every device.
