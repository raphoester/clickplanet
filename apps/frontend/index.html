ClickPlanet

ClickPlanet is a free multiplayer game in your browser. Pick a country, then click tiles on a 3D globe to paint them with its flag. Everyone plays on the same planet, live, and each country fights for the most ground.

You do not need an account to play. You can sign in with Google or Discord to keep your account on every device.
